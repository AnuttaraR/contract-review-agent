# Blacklisted Contract Terms & Red Flag Clauses

Version: 2.3 | Effective: 2026-02-01 | Owner: Legal Team

## Category A — Automatic Rejection Triggers

Contracts containing ANY of these terms must be REJECTED and escalated to Legal:

### Financial Red Flags
- "unlimited liability" on the client's side
- "non-refundable" deposits exceeding 30% of total contract value
- "advance payment" of 100% before delivery commencement
- Payment escalation clauses with no cap (e.g., "at vendor's discretion")
- "Most Favored Nation" pricing that obligates us without reciprocal benefit

### Lock-in & Control Red Flags
- "exclusive supplier" or "exclusivity clause" without board-level approval
- "non-compete" clauses restricting our ability to work with other vendors in the space
- "assignment" restrictions that prevent us from assigning the contract in M&A scenarios
- "change of control" termination rights held exclusively by vendor (we must retain same right)

### IP & Data Red Flags
- Vendor claims ownership of data we provide or that is generated from our data
- "perpetual, royalty-free license" granted to vendor over our proprietary data
- IP developed under the contract defaults to vendor ownership
- Restrictions on data portability or right to export

### Regulatory Red Flags
- Gag clauses preventing disclosure to regulators (illegal in most jurisdictions)
- Clauses waiving our right to file regulatory complaints
- Indemnification of vendor for their regulatory violations (they must indemnify us, not the reverse)
- Data transfer to jurisdictions on sanctions lists

## Category B — Requires VP/Legal Approval Before Proceeding

### Pricing Red Flags
- Automatic price escalation > 5% per annum without renegotiation right
- Price changes with < 30-day notice
- Retroactive billing or billing for undisputed amounts past 12 months
- "True-up" clauses with no cap on additional charges

### Operational Red Flags
- Vendor unilateral right to modify service terms without notice
- Scheduled maintenance windows > 8 hours/month without SLA credit
- No-fault termination liability (we pay termination fees even when vendor breaches)
- Limitation on our ability to perform audits or security assessments

### Liability Red Flags
- Liability cap set at amount lower than 1x annual contract value
- Exclusion of liability for gross negligence or willful misconduct
- No indemnification clause for vendor's IP infringement
- Mutual limitation of consequential damages where vendor handles our critical systems

## Category C — Flag for Review (Legal Discretion)

These terms should be noted but may be acceptable with appropriate context:

- Choice of law in jurisdictions outside our preferred list (Category C if FATF-compliant country)
- Governing language not English (acceptable if certified translation provided)
- Currency other than USD/EUR/GBP (acceptable with hedging provisions)
- Force majeure clauses that are overly broad (pandemic language added 2024–2025 still acceptable)
- "Time is of the essence" clauses (review delivery schedules carefully)
- Third-party beneficiary clauses

## Red Flag Scanning Keywords

The AI extraction system should flag the following keywords for human review:

**Financial:**
unlimited, non-refundable, advance, prepay, escalation, surcharge, additional fees at discretion

**Control:**
exclusive, exclusivity, sole supplier, non-compete, assignment restriction, change of control

**IP/Data:**
perpetual license to vendor, vendor owns, work-for-hire to vendor, data may be used for, data processing for vendor's benefit

**Regulatory:**
confidential to regulators, waive right to report, not disclose to authorities, gag, muzzle

**Liability:**
no liability, unlimited client liability, cap at zero, consequential damages excluded in all cases

**Operational:**
vendor may modify, at vendor's sole discretion, no audit rights, no testing rights

## Escalation Protocol for Blacklisted Terms

1. AI system flags contract with blacklisted term → NEEDS_REVIEW status
2. Legal team receives notification within 1 hour
3. Legal reviews within 1 business day
4. If Category A: Escalate to General Counsel within 24 hours
5. Document decision and rationale in contract record
6. If rejected: Standard rejection letter to vendor with 30-day resubmission window
