# Internal Contract Policies & Standards

Version: 3.2 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Legal & Procurement

## 1. Financial Limits & Approval Matrix

| Contract Value | Approval Required | Payment Terms Required |
|---|---|---|
| < $10,000 | Team Manager | Net-30 or better |
| $10,000 – $100,000 | Department Head | Net-30 minimum |
| $100,001 – $500,000 | VP + Legal Review | Net-30 mandatory |
| > $500,000 | C-Suite + Board | Net-30, milestone payments preferred |
| Any value with auto-renewal | VP approval | Must include termination notice clause |

## 2. Payment Terms Standards

ACCEPTABLE payment terms:
- Net-30 (preferred standard)
- Net-45 (acceptable with justification)
- Net-60 (requires VP approval)
- Milestone-based payments (must define clear deliverable milestones)

UNACCEPTABLE payment terms:
- Net-7 or Net-14 (too aggressive — creates cash flow risk)
- Advance payment > 30% of contract value without milestone
- Non-refundable deposits > $50,000 without legal sign-off

## 3. Liability Cap Requirements

- Minimum liability cap: 1x annual contract value
- Standard liability cap: 2x annual contract value (recommended)
- Maximum uncapped liability: NOT permitted without board approval
- Mutual indemnification: Required for contracts > $100,000
- IP indemnification: Required for all software/SaaS contracts

## 4. Contract Duration & Renewal

- Maximum initial term: 3 years without board review
- Auto-renewal clauses: Must include at least 60-day notice period to opt out
- Notice period minimum: 30 days
- Notice period for contracts > $500K: 90 days minimum

## 5. Data & Privacy Requirements

- GDPR compliance: Mandatory for all vendors processing EU personal data
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA): Required if vendor handles ANY personal data
- Data residency: EU data must remain in EU or approved jurisdictions (UK, Canada, Japan, Australia)
- SOC2 Type II: Required for cloud/SaaS vendors processing sensitive data
- Security breach notification: Vendor must notify within 72 hours

## 6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

Preferred governing law jurisdictions (in order of preference):
1. England & Wales
2. New York, USA
3. Singapore
4. Germany (for EU-only vendors)

Unacceptable jurisdictions:
- Any jurisdiction on FATF grey/black list
- Jurisdictions without reciprocal enforcement treaties with our operating countries

Dispute resolution preference: Mediation first, then arbitration (ICC or LCIA)
Avoid: Exclusive court jurisdiction clauses that prevent arbitration

## 7. SLA Requirements

All service agreements must meet minimum SLA standards:
- Uptime guarantee: Minimum 99.5% (preferred: 99.9%)
- Critical incident response: Within 4 hours
- Standard incident response: Within 24 hours
- Penalty clause: Required for contracts > $50,000 (minimum 5% of monthly fee per SLA breach)
- Scheduled maintenance windows: Must be communicated 48 hours in advance

## 8. Termination Rights

REQUIRED in all contracts:
- Termination for cause (material breach, insolvency, regulatory violation)
- Termination for convenience (company must retain right to exit)

Minimum notice periods:
- Contracts < $50K: 30 days
- Contracts $50K–$250K: 60 days
- Contracts > $250K: 90 days

NOT ACCEPTABLE:
- Lock-in clauses preventing termination for convenience
- Early termination penalties > 3 months remaining fees
- Auto-renewal without termination right

## 9. IP Ownership Policy

For custom development / work-for-hire:
- All developed IP must be owned by our company
- Vendor retains license to background IP only
- Escrow of source code required for software > $200K total value

For SaaS / licensed software:
- Perpetual license right to data exports (no vendor lock-in)
- Right to audit usage and data processing

## 10. Blacklisted Contract Terms

The following terms automatically flag a contract for rejection review:
- "Unlimited liability" provisions on our side
- "Exclusive supplier" clauses without board approval
- "No-poach" clauses covering > 12 months
- "Most favored nation" pricing without reciprocal benefit
- Automatic price escalation > 5% per year without cap
- Waiver of jury trial in jurisdictions where this is unusual
- Unilateral amendment rights by vendor
- Gag clauses preventing disclosure of contract existence to regulators
